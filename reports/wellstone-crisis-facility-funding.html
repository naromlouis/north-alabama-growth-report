Wellstone funding: what the commitment supports next

The City’s opioid-settlement commitment supports a crisis facility, with annual limits and a future funding start.

NAGR · Sources checked November 8, 2026

Huntsville’s October 22 announcement commits up to $3 million in opioid-settlement funds to Wellstone, capped at $1 million per year beginning in fiscal year 2028. The City described a 16-bed mental health crisis facility under construction.

The funding is intended for furnishings, equipment and professional staff salaries after opening. The same announcement provides up to $120,000 for Not One More Alabama’s mobile education initiative. Approved funding limits should not be read as amounts already paid.

What it means locally

NAGR perspective

The next milestones to watch are the new facility’s opening and implementation of the funding commitments. The City’s funding announcement does not provide an opening date. Wellstone’s existing services are separate from the newly funded facility.
