Nearly 828 acres rezoned. What does that allow next?

Two Huntsville land-use decisions establish permitted uses, with housing, preservation and commercial possibilities in view.

NAGR · Sources checked November 8, 2026

Huntsville’s October 22 announcement covers two zoning decisions totaling 827.98 acres. At Cleary Farm in east Huntsville, 536.68 acres received residential zoning. The City says developers plan single-family homes while preserving much of the land.

A separate 291.3-acre property west of County Line Road and south of I-565 was rezoned for potential retail and industrial warehouse uses. These decisions define allowable uses; they do not establish that construction has started.

What it means locally

NAGR perspective

Zoning is one step in a longer development process. Future plans, infrastructure commitments and construction announcements will help show how these properties actually change. Approval of a land use is distinct from completed development.
