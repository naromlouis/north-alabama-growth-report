Two road investments to watch after the $14.3 million announcement

Slaughter Road and Resolute Way remain separate projects with different funding commitments and next steps.

NAGR · Sources checked November 8, 2026

An October 20 report from 256 Today identified $3.5 million for additional lanes on Slaughter Road and a $10.8 million federal grant for Resolute Way. Together, the commitments total $14.3 million.

Resolute Way is a planned connection serving Redstone Arsenal’s Gate 9. Slaughter Road improvements address an existing corridor shared by multiple jurisdictions. The funding announcement is not evidence that either project is complete.

What it means locally

NAGR perspective

The next questions are which work the funding covers, when project milestones are announced and how commuters will be affected. The October funding report establishes the awards, rather than a new construction start or opening date.
