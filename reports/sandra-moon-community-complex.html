Sandra Moon is open. Here’s what South Huntsville gained.

Arts, adaptive recreation and gathering spaces give the former Grissom campus a new role in South Huntsville.

NAGR · Sources checked November 8, 2026

South Huntsville’s Sandra Moon Community Complex is open. The City’s current facility page confirms that residents can use the complex, following its September 15, 2026 grand opening.

The Bailey Cove Road complex brings together a performing arts center, adaptive recreation spaces, an event hall, a community meeting room and the South Huntsville Public Library. The City also lists courts, fields, gymnasiums and an accessible playground among its amenities.

What it means locally

NAGR perspective

A shared venue can create opportunities for community programming, local partnerships and family events. Those are potential uses, not confirmed vendor bookings or announced festivals. The City’s current program guide and rental information explain what is available.
